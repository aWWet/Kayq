Պատմություն — The House

A room that was never for the family alone.

In the old highland houses of Lori, the vernatun sat a step above the rest — the one room facing south, the one with the good rugs, opened whenever someone came down from the hills or up from the valley. It wasn't for everyday. It was for whoever showed up hungry.

We took the name straight from that habit. Stone walls, a slow fire, bread that doesn't stop coming, and a chair pulled out before you've finished saying hello. Vanadzor sits low in the Lori basin, ringed by the same volcanic tuff this house is built from — we didn't have to look far for a character to build around.
